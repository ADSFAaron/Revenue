Privacy Policy

Revenue · com.adsf.revenue
Last updated: 31 August 2026

Revenue is a till and bookkeeping app for small food businesses. This policy describes what it stores, where it goes, and how to get rid of it. It covers the Android app and the web version at revenueapp-b8849.web.app.

The short version. Revenue holds the account you sign in with and the trading records you enter — your menu, your orders, your takings. There is no advertising, no analytics, and nothing is sold or shared with anyone for marketing. Photographs of a menu are sent to Google's Gemini API to be read into dishes and are not kept afterwards. You can delete your account, and everything belonging to it, from inside the app.

What is collected, and why

Your account

Email address Identifies the account and is how you sign in and recover it.
Display name Shown to colleagues in the same store, and recorded against changes you make so a manager can see who did what.
Password Handled entirely by Google Firebase Authentication. Revenue never sees or stores it.
Google sign-in If you choose it, Google supplies your email address and name. Nothing else is requested.
Passkeys If you register one, the server stores its public key, a signature counter, and a device label you can read. A passkey's private key never leaves your device, and biometric data is never transmitted — your fingerprint or face is checked by the phone itself.

Your store's trading records

Everything you enter in order to run the shop: the store's name and settings (tax, trading-day cutoff, payment methods, delivery platforms and their commission rates), the menu (dish names, prices, ingredient costs, categories), orders (items, quantities, prices, payment method, guest count, when they were rung up and by whom), the daily totals derived from them, and a change log of edits, voids and price changes.

This is the app's purpose rather than a by-product of it. It belongs to the store, and it is visible to the people who work there according to their role — staff, manager or owner.

Photographs of a menu

If you use the menu importer, the photographs you take or choose are sent to Google's Gemini API (generativelanguage.googleapis.com) to be read into a list of dishes and prices, which you then confirm before anything is saved. Up to four photographs per import, downsampled on the device first.

Revenue does not store the photographs. They are held in memory for the length of the request and are gone when it finishes. Google's handling of them is governed by the Gemini API terms.

The camera permission is only ever used for this. There is no other camera feature, and the app has no microphone, location, contacts or file-library access at all.

Feedback you send

If you use Send feedback, the message is stored along with your account id, your store id, and the app version — so a report can be reproduced and answered. Nothing is sent unless you press send.

What stays on your device

Two things, and only these: your light/dark preference, and any orders you rang up while offline, held until the connection returns and they can be sent. Device backup and device-to-device transfer are both switched off for this app, so neither leaves the phone by any route other than reaching your store's records.

App integrity

Revenue uses Google Play Integrity (Android) and reCAPTCHA Enterprise (web) to check that requests come from a genuine copy of the app rather than from a script. These are Google services and receive device and browser integrity signals. They are not used to identify you and Revenue receives no information from them beyond pass or fail.

What is not collected

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or usage tracking. The app contains no analytics SDK.
  • No crash reporting.
  • No location, contacts, calendar, call or message data.
  • No access to your photo library, files or documents.
  • Nothing is sold, rented, or shared with third parties for their own purposes.

Where it is kept

Revenue runs on Google Firebase. Your account is held by Firebase Authentication; your store's records are in Cloud Firestore in Google's asia-east2 region (Hong Kong); the server functions run in asia-east1 (Taiwan). Google acts as a processor on our behalf and does not use the data for its own purposes.

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by Google Cloud. Who may read what is enforced on the server by security rules, not by the app: a member of staff can only ever reach their own store's data, and only the parts their role allows.

How long it is kept

Trading records are kept for as long as the store exists, because they are the store's books. Orders are never deleted — a cancelled sale is marked void and stays visible, which is what makes a till discrepancy arguable. Invite codes expire after 30 minutes. Menu photographs are not retained at all.

Deleting your account

In the app: Store → Account & app → Delete account.

  • If you are staff or a manager, your membership and your login are removed. The orders you rang up stay with the store — they are its books, not your personal data.
  • If you are the owner, the store goes with you: the menu, every order, the daily totals, the change log, the invite codes, and every colleague's login for that store. This is irreversible, and the app asks you to type the store's name to confirm it. It works this way because ownership cannot be transferred — a store whose owner vanished could never be administered again.

Deletion is immediate and permanent. There is no recovery window and no archived copy.

Your rights

You can see everything held about your store from inside the app, and export your trading records to an Excel workbook at any time (Reports → Export). You can correct anything you entered. You can delete your account as described above. If you would like a copy of what is held, a correction we have not provided a screen for, or the erasure of something specific, write to the address below.

Children

Revenue is a tool for running a business. It is not directed at children and is not designed or marketed for anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects what is collected or who it goes to, the date at the top will change and the new version will be published here before the change takes effect.
