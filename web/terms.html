What Revenue is

Revenue records sales for a small food business and reports on them: a menu, orders, daily totals, and analysis of what sells and what earns. It is a tool for keeping your own records.

It is not an accounting service, a tax filing service, a payment processor, or professional advice of any kind. It does not send anything to a tax authority, does not handle money, and does not replace whatever your business is legally required to keep. Meeting those obligations remains yours.

Your account, and your staff

You are responsible for who can get into your store's data. In practice that means:

  • Keeping your own sign-in credentials to yourself.
  • Issuing invite codes only to people who should have access, and revoking ones that were read out to the wrong person. Codes are single-use and expire, but a code in the wrong hands before it expires is a way in.
  • Setting each colleague's role — staff, manager or owner — to what they actually need.
  • Choosing whether to share one login across a counter. The app records which account rang up each order; if several people use one account, that record cannot tell them apart, and that is a consequence of the choice rather than a fault in the app.

Actions taken by anyone signed into your store count as taken by your store.

Your records, and keeping your own copy

The trading records you enter are yours. We claim no ownership of them and do not sell or share them — see the Privacy Policy for what is held and where it goes.

Keep your own copies. The app can export your trading records to an Excel workbook at any time (Reports → Export), and we recommend doing so on a schedule that suits how much a lost month would cost you. Your records live in Google Firebase and are as durable as that service, which is durable but is not a promise, and there is no separate archive we can restore from.

Deleting your account deletes data permanently and immediately. If you are the owner, it takes the whole store with it — every order, the menu, the daily totals and every colleague's login. There is no recovery window and no archived copy. The app asks you to type the store's name first because this cannot be undone.

What is not guaranteed

We try to keep this working. We cannot promise the following, and you should plan for them:

  • Availability. The service depends on Google Firebase and on your own connection. Outages, maintenance and network failures happen, and some can stop you ringing up a sale.
  • Orders taken offline live on one device. When there is no connection, an order is saved on that phone or tablet and sent when the connection returns. It is never synced anywhere else in the meantime. If that device is lost, reset, or the app is uninstalled before the queue has drained, those orders are gone and we cannot recover them. The app shows how many are waiting; do not leave a queue sitting.
  • Reading a menu from a photograph. The menu importer uses an AI model and can misread a price, a name or a whole line. That is why it shows you what it read and asks you to confirm before anything is saved. Check it.
  • Figures you did not enter correctly. Every total, cost and margin is computed from what was typed in. A wrong ingredient cost produces a confident wrong profit.

The analysis is information, not advice

The Insights screens classify dishes, describe when you are busy and suggest what tends to be ordered together. These are calculations over your own sales, presented to help you think. They are not business, financial or professional advice, they depend on how much data you have and how complete your ingredient costs are, and decisions you make from them are yours.
